Topic map

Topics tile together like a disk-space map. A topic's area is its share of the work, so the biggest block is where most of your open tasks are. Each small cell inside is one task. The topics shown are sample placeholders.

Try it

  • Start typing anywhere to add a task: Call the bank @ Admin. After @, pick a topic with ↑ ↓ and Tab
  • Tick a task's checkbox; the map re-balances
  • Click + on a topic to add tasks, Enter to keep adding
  • Drag a topic's header onto another topic to group them
  • Drag a topic onto the top bar to take it out of its group
  • Double-click a tile to zoom in; small cells show their text as you zoom
  • Drag the map to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Double-click a topic or group name to rename it
  • Click any colour dot (on a topic, a group, or in the List) to change its colour
  • Select a topic and press Delete to remove it
